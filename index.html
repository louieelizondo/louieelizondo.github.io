I run Natural Balance — homestyle meals in Chihuahua: prepped, cooked, refrigerated, frozen, and hot-ready combos for families who want to eat well without the daily cooking grind.

Instead of running it the traditional way, I rebuilt operations around software. Notion runs the kitchen brain. Shopify runs sales. Then I started writing the tools no vendor would sell us — payroll bonuses, contracts, a six-app finance suite.

This domain is the public log of that work. Every project started as a real problem on a real line. I am still learning. The point is to keep plating.

Build it. Break it. Feel like an idiot. Figure it out. Do it again — but better. — Louie Elizondo
